# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude when working in this repo.

## What this repo is

A planning space, not a codebase. Charles and Chris are brainstorming a possible new undergraduate
major (AI, or AI / Software Development) in the School of Engineering & Technology at UW Tacoma.
Content is Markdown: ideas, research notes, meeting agendas, open questions, and decisions.

Audience for most documents: CS faculty colleagues and, later, curriculum committees and
administrators. Write for smart readers who are not yet bought in.

## Two contributors

Charles and Chris both contribute. Don't assume an idea belongs to whoever is in the current
session. When an idea file has an author, preserve it. When two views differ, record both in a
"Discussion" section rather than picking one.

## Conventions

- **Ideas:** one file per idea in `ideas/`, kebab-case filename, based on `ideas/_template.md`.
- **Meetings:** `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
- **Decisions:** append to `decisions.md` with date, decision, and reasoning. Never rewrite past
  entries; supersede them with a new entry.
- **Open questions:** live in `open-questions.md`. When one is resolved, move it to a "Resolved"
  section with a pointer to the decision.
- Use absolute dates (`2026-10-23`), not "next Friday".

## Accuracy

- Do not invent facts about UW, UW Tacoma, SET, existing courses, enrollment, or peer programs.
  When research is needed, cite the source (URL and access date) in the `research/` file.
- Mark speculation and estimates clearly.
- Distinguish "we decided" from "we're considering". Most things here are the second.

## Git

Don't commit or push unless asked.
