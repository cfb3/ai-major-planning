## A B.A. in Artificial Intelligence

Charles and Chris converged on a **Bachelor of Arts in Artificial Intelligence** (still being
considered, not decided). It refines the two-degree idea from
[2026-10-22](2026-10-22-charles-chris-tom.md):

- **Reshape the existing B.A.** into the AI degree (or create a new B.A.; Charles left this open
  after the call); the **B.S. stays the research and
  grad-school track**. That avoids building a new program from scratch.
- **Philosophy: "creating with AI."** Practitioners and creators, not researchers: software,
  games, books, multimedia. Example: a graduate student using Claude and Gemini in a pipeline to
  make sprites for an educational math game.
- **Practice-oriented and portfolio-based.** No calculus; no heavy programming; portfolios
  instead of exams.
- **Name:** "AI" needs to be in front for students and employers. "Software dev with AI" was
  judged too narrow; "B.A. in Artificial Intelligence" is broad enough to cover everything planned.

**Curriculum blocks under discussion:**

| Block | Size | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Programming literacy | 1 course | Read code and file structures; modularization, decomposition, state, repetition |
| Software development with AI | 2–3 courses | How systems fit together: web, mobile, peer-to-peer, distributed |
| Data | 1 course | Data formats, databases, basic data theory (no SQL or ER diagrams required) |
| Design, UI/UX and creativity | 1–2 courses | Design thinking, usable interfaces, creative work with AI (added by Charles after the call) |
| Networking, security | 1 each? | Practical, systems-level understanding |
| QA and testing | 2–3 courses | Testing methods, reading and writing specs, quality assurance |
| Ethics, tech and society | ? | AI ethics, environmental impact, labor; partner with SIAS faculty |
| Senior design capstone | 2–3 courses | Team builds a real product; brings the blocks together (added by Charles after the call) |
| Open block | ? | Left open for colleagues' suggestions (added by Charles after the call) |
| Required minor | a minor | From another unit: business (Milgard), GIS, environmental science, and others |

- **AI access:** one Claude Team account per student for the whole program, instead of separate
  per-course fee accounts as AI spreads across courses. The recap mentions the $100/month tier.
- **On "how do you know AI code works?":** human-written code is rarely held to that standard
  either; if anything, use AI to double-check human review.
