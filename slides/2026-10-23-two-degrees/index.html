One program, two degrees

Ideas from Charles and Chris for Computer Science at UW Tacoma, for discussion, not a mandate

Our CS degree already works as a service degree. We could make that explicit.

  • Most graduates go into industry software jobs, and about a quarter head to grad school or research
  • One set of requirements tries to serve both groups
  • AI tools now do much of the programming, which changes what each group needs
  • Enrollment is expected to drop further in 2027–28
