# Observed Gaps from Student Demos

- **Author:** Charles and Chris
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Type:** other (evidence)
- **Maturity:** sketch

## The idea

Students built full applications with AI assistance in a short window and demoed them on
2026-10-19. What they *couldn't* do shows what a program needs to teach.

## Gaps observed

1. **Where state lives.** Apps saved state "just in the browser." This is domain knowledge that
   vibe coding misses, and that even senior CS students may lack if they have never built a
   distributed application.
2. **UI/UX.** Apps looked good but had mediocre usability.
3. **Finishing.** Nearly every demo had small parts that didn't work or weren't done. In
   production software, cleaning these up takes the most time. (Some of this may come from the
   short timeline.)
4. **Testing (to confirm).** We don't know if any project included formal testing.

## Why it matters

This is concrete local evidence for Friday's 2026-10-23 meeting, and the basis for the
[curriculum skeleton](curriculum-skeleton.md).

## Ways to get more data

- **Pilot with non-majors or 142-level students** to see which material can be paired with the
  major and which must stay in.
- **Interview students in the class who are new to the major** about their experience.
- **Check the projects for tests** to confirm or rule out gap 4.

## Open questions / risks

- How much of gaps 2–3 comes from the time limit rather than missing knowledge?
- Small sample, one course. Treat as illustrative, not conclusive.
